Classic Companion iOS Support

Classic Companion is a reading application for iPhone and iPad, created for calm, focused reading of timeless classical works. It presents carefully prepared texts with clear navigation and reading aids designed to support, rather than interrupt, the reading experience.

Basic Use

  1. Open Classic Companion.
  2. Browse the available classics and select a work.
  3. Use the table of contents to enter a chapter or section.
  4. Read in the dedicated reading view and continue through the text at your own pace.
  5. Use available reading aids, such as pronunciation guidance or contextual word notes, when needed.

Reading Experience

Classic Companion is designed to keep attention on the text itself. Typography, spacing, navigation, and supporting information are presented with clarity and restraint for a quiet reading experience.

Content and Reading Aids

Classical texts and related reading aids are carefully prepared for the application. Reading aids are provided as supplementary references and are not intended to replace authoritative scholarly editions or professional academic research.

Troubleshooting

  • If a text does not display as expected, close and reopen the application.
  • Ensure that your device is running a supported version of iOS or iPadOS.
  • If an issue persists, include the title of the classic, the relevant chapter or passage, and any visible error information when contacting support.

Support Contact

Please use the support contact method provided in the App Store listing and include your device model, iOS/iPadOS version, Classic Companion version, and a description of the issue.
